Science is most useful when people can participate in it. My outreach spans community sustainability education, student mentoring, public science events, and practical computing workshops.

## Community sustainability

### Keney Park Sustainability Project (KPSP)

My work with the [Keney Park Sustainability Project (KPSP)](https://www.keneyparksustainability.org/), based in Hartford, CT, has supported its beekeeping initiative. KPSP is a non-profit organization located in a federally designated Promise Zone. They aim to provide education about sustainable practices to the surrounding urban community through hands-on programs. The beekeeping initiative connects hands-on learning with honey production and community education workshops.

## Science education & mentoring

### Mentoring Student Diversity Organizations at the University of Rhode Island

As a first-generation student and woman in science, I am dedicated to integrating diversity, equity, inclusion, and justice (DEIJ) into all aspects of my work. At the University of Rhode Island, I've been fortunate to directly mentor student members of diversity groups, such as the [Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers](https://web.uri.edu/engineering/student-resources/undergraduate-students/student-organizations/society-of-hispanic-professional-engineers-shpe/). If you are a student involved with a diversity group and would like to coordinate a workshop or talk, please don't hesitate to reach out!

### Chair, Outreach Committee, Science of Learning and Art of Communication NSF Research Traineeship (NRT) Program

I'm currently serving as Chair of the Outreach Committee for an [NSF Research Traineeship program (SLAC)](https://slac.uconn.edu/) at the University of Connecticut. We create accessible scientific resources and plan events aimed at engaging the community in STEM education. We focus on inspiring STEM interest early in the lives of children and in historically marginalized populations. Most recently we have been collaborating with the Connecticut Science Center for their [STEM Career Showcases](https://ctsciencecenter.org/stem-career-showcases/), allowing us to give Middle and High School students the opportunity to learn about various careers and ask questions.

### Software Carpentry

As a certified, volunteer instructor for a non-profit organization called [Software Carpentry](https://software-carpentry.org/), I work alongside other data science experts to give free (or minimal cost) coding workshops to university students and researchers. As a former Software Carpentry attendee, I am delighted to give back and pass on this knowledge to others.

### Beekeeper Mentoring

Mentoring new beekeepers is something I also enjoy immensely. If you're interested in getting started, look for local beekeeping associations in your area or reach out to me with questions!

### Ask a Brain Scientist

I worked closely with Dr. Fumiko Hoeft on her [Ask a Brain Scientist](http://haskinsglobal.org/ask-a-brain-scientist/) series, where kids aged 8-13 can attend, learn, ask questions, and receive a Junior Neuroscientist certificate for their participation.
